## Model outputs kept as research records

The measurement records contain text generated by AI models. They are published so that every verdict can be
checked:
- `eval/` holds raw model answers and judge outputs;
- `majelis/` holds answers from a multi-model council.

The models include:
- the author's own fine-tunes;
- open models (Qwen, DeepSeek and others);
- commercial AI services used as benchmark subjects or as judges.

This repository **grants no right to use that model-generated text to train AI models.** The terms of the
service that produced the text still apply. It is published only as evidence for the experiments it belongs
to.
