# Paket Prompt Gambar — Kandungan Migan

Empat gambar konsep dibuat dengan alat pembuat gambar bawaan dan disimpan di `konsep-gambar/`. Gambar adalah acuan suasana, bukan aset runtime; prototipe 3D tetap prosedural dan offline.

## Kunci visual global

Gunakan kunci berikut pada semua wilayah agar satu dunia terasa konsisten:

> Fantasi abstrak terlukis, stylized low-poly painterly concept art, bukan fotoreal. Batu abu tua berlumut, hijau hutan gelap, cahaya kristal sian-toska, aksen emas-oranye senja, kabut berlapis, bentuk besar dan terbaca, atmosfer sakral namun UI-serius. Rune adalah tanda geometris dekoratif, bukan alfabet yang harus terbaca. Skala manusia kecil menegaskan landmark kolosal. Tanpa teks UI, logo, watermark, merek, fotorealisme, kota modern, senjata realistis, atau detail mikro yang bising.

Palet tetap: `#050d0a`, `#0d1f1a`, `#2fe39a`, `#7ef0bd`, `#4fc3cf`, `#ff8a24`, `#ffc15c`, batu abu kehijauan dan kabut biru kelabu.

## Prompt per wilayah

### 1. Peta Kandungan — panorama seluruh dunia

> Wide aerial key art of the embryonic world of Migan, a compact open-world fantasy island floating in mist. At the center, an ancient dark forest shelters a glass sphere wrapped in dry roots, holding a dim cyan-green winged spirit embryo. Around it: a broken floating stone gate with a luminous green-cyan crystal pyramid, a field of giant mossy rune swords, golden grassland with crystal obelisks, a winding turquoise retrieval river, a colossal mountain ring portal, outer cliff islands with mossy rings, a pilgrim mission board, a forge, a circular training arena, and a stone conversation pavilion. Three tiny robed pilgrims follow curved paths. Painterly stylized low-poly concept art, dramatic cloud layers, readable landmark silhouettes, deep green and charcoal foundation, cyan-turquoise light, restrained sunset orange-gold accents. No labels, no UI, no logo, no watermark, no photorealism.

### 2. Hutan Kandungan — embrio ruh Migan

> Hero concept art inside an ancient moss forest: a large levitating glass sphere tightly cradled by sculptural dry tree roots. Inside, a dim cyan-turquoise nebula swirls around a tiny winged spirit embryo asleep and not yet born. The glow must feel subdued, uncertain, and dormant—never triumphant and never like a progress meter. Bright moss, small bioluminescent mushrooms, thin golden dust, soft god rays through cold fog. In front, a small stone pavilion for conversation faces the sphere, with one robed pilgrim for scale. Painterly fantasy, stylized low-poly forms, sacred but legible, dark forest-green and charcoal, cyan core, restrained amber rim light. No text, no interface, no watermark, no photorealism.

### 3. Lembah Gerbang Patah — riset dan pra-daftar

> A broken ancient stone arch floats in two separated curved pieces above an alpine green valley and turquoise lake. Weathered stone wings and geometric rune grooves cover the arch. Between the fragments levitates a luminous green-cyan crystal pyramid, clearly an instrument of measurement and judgment. Three small robed pilgrims walk a winding trail toward it; distant eagle silhouettes cross dramatic overcast clouds. Moss, mist, soft painted light, monumental readable shapes, stylized fantasy concept art with low-poly discipline. Grey mossy stone, deep green, cyan glow, small sunset-gold accents. No readable text, no modern objects, no logo, no watermark, no photorealism.

### 4. Padang Pedang Rune — hukum dan temuan

> A vast rolling green field filled with dozens of colossal ancient stone swords embedded upright at varied angles. Every blade has one simple luminous rune-ID mark, presented as abstract geometry rather than readable language. Moss climbs the blades; a winding dirt trail lets a tiny robed scholar inspect them. Ghibli-like painted atmosphere with disciplined low-poly silhouettes, clear foreground-midground-background rhythm, blue sky breaking through mist, cyan rune light and restrained gold sunlight. The scene should feel like a library of laws and findings, not a battlefield. No violence, no blood, no readable words, no watermark, no photorealism.

### 5. Padang Obelisk Emas + Sungai Toska — silsilah dan retrieval

> Golden grassland at late afternoon containing twelve distinct crystal obelisks on low stone plinths, each representing a model and condition. A glittering turquoise river curves between them like a visible retrieval flow. Small square honesty plots surround selected obelisks; patches of warm light move across the grass. One distant robed traveler studies an obelisk. Painterly stylized fantasy, strong readable silhouettes, mossy stone, translucent cyan crystals, deep green shadows, restrained orange-gold sunset. No graphs, no progress bars, no text, no logos, no watermark, no photorealism.

### 6. Puncak Cincin — gerbang pindah cepat

> A colossal circular stone ring stands on a high mountain ridge, its edge catching a sharp line of golden sun while the opening contains quiet cyan mist. A tiny traveler approaches along a narrow path. Below are layered fog mountains and hints of the central forest. The ring is ancient, mossy, broken in places, and functions as a solemn evaluation gate and fast-travel portal. Painterly low-poly fantasy key art, monumental scale, grey-green stone, cool mist, cyan portal, orange-gold rim light. No sci-fi machinery, no text, no watermark, no photorealism.

### 7. Kepulauan Cincin — data dan aset

> Several small lush cliff islands rise from a turquoise sea, linked visually by repeated moss-covered stone arches and rings. Some islands hold clearly complete stone repositories; others are empty or hidden by fog, honestly signaling assets not found. A huge pale moon or planet hangs behind layered clouds, with birds and one tiny robed pilgrim crossing a narrow bridge. Painterly stylized low-poly fantasy, airy but mysterious, green moss, grey stone, turquoise water, pale cyan sky, sparse gold highlights. No treasure piles, no fake abundance, no text, no watermark, no photorealism.

### 8. Balai Bicara — chat dan konteks retrieval

> A readable stone conversation pavilion directly facing the root-wrapped spirit embryo in the forest. Inside are two balanced work bays for model comparison, a central empty speaking desk, and several floating rune slabs representing retrieved local documents; each slab has only abstract geometric marks and a small cyan relevance glow. A gentle guide-avatar silhouette stands beside the desk, while the sleeping embryo outside emits a restrained pulse. Combine fantasy sanctuary architecture with the clarity of a serious desktop AI workspace, without showing literal UI screens. Dark green-charcoal stone, moss, cyan-turquoise retrieval light, thin orange-gold guidance accents, painterly low-poly style. No fake answer text, no brand logos, no watermark, no photorealism.

### 9. Papan Misi, Tempa, Arena, dan Majelis — vignette pendukung

> Four-panel environment concept sheet in one coherent visual language: a carved pilgrim mission monolith with exactly twenty-two small unfilled/filled notch marks; a compact stone forge with three distinct hearth stones for Laptop, Bmax, and VPS-2 but no written labels; a circular training arena for teaching and model comparison; and an honest empty council circle of monoliths. Painterly stylized low-poly fantasy, consistent mossy grey stone, deep green, cyan measurement light, restrained orange fire, clean negative space, readable silhouettes. No readable text, no logo, no watermark, no photorealism.

## Aturan seleksi hasil

1. Landmark harus terbaca pada thumbnail 320 px tanpa bantuan teks.
2. Embrio selalu redup/tertidur sampai A4 memiliki syarat nyata; tolak hasil yang terlihat sudah lahir atau menang.
3. Rune boleh berpendar tetapi tidak boleh menyerupai tulisan data palsu.
4. Aksen oranye maksimal sebagai penunjuk/kehangatan; sian tetap menandai model, pengukuran, dan retrieval.
5. Peziarah menjaga skala dan arah pandang, bukan menjadi potret karakter utama.
6. Hindari detail yang mustahil direalisasikan dalam anggaran low-poly prototipe.
